What the business gets

  • The record. Who sent what to which AI tool, by account, site and time. ChatGPT Business and Claude Team give you no live log; only a manual export after the fact.
  • Brakes. Credentials blocked outright. Health, payment and personal data held with the person deciding, and the approval recorded.
  • Rules in plain language. A clinic allows patient data with approval; a construction firm blocks it. Each class of data has its own rule and its own wording, changed on a web page, live on the next message.
  • A view the owner can read. Red, yellow or green, with the counts behind each, for the monthly review.

Built for MSPs

  • One stack, a sealed tenant per client, owner and viewer roles.
  • Hosted by Sentinel and managed for you, hosted on your own instance, or run in the client's network. You own the client relationship either way.
  • Alerts to Slack, PagerDuty, Teams or any webhook, carrying the finding, host and path, never the prompt.

What it does not do, so you know before the pilot

Prompt-injection phrasings outside the current pattern set are not caught. Model replies in the browser are not inspected. The extension covers Chrome and Edge on chatgpt.com and claude.ai; desktop and mobile apps are not covered. A user with developer tools can disable the extension on their own machine.
